05 / PERMISSIONLESS MARKETING

WE DON'T WAIT
FOR BRANDS
TO HIRE US.

We are going to find brands and companies we believe deserve attention. Then we are going to create campaigns for them.

Not because they asked us to. Because we have an idea worth showing.

We will develop concepts, create the work, and put it in front of the world. If the brand likes what we do, maybe they hire us. If they don't, we still have experience, a campaign to show, and another reason to keep going.

THIS IS PERMISSIONLESS MARKETING.

Bold, creative and ambitious—but responsible. No harassment, no deception, no unauthorized use of private information, and no pretending to represent a brand without permission.

06 / THE HONEST BIT

WE DON'T HAVE MONEY TO GIVE YOU.
WE HAVE WORK TO DO.

This is an unpaid creative community. There is no salary, stipend, or guaranteed financial compensation at the moment.

What you can gain:

  • Real project experience and portfolio work.
  • Collaboration with other creatives.
  • Exposure to industries and disciplines.
  • Space to learn and improve your craft.
  • A network to help execute ideas.
  • A chance to make campaigns that get attention.
  • Potential future opportunities from the work and relationships you build.

We do not promise jobs, money, or guaranteed career outcomes. We promise a room where doing the work matters.

09 / FAQS

ASK
ANYTHING.

IS THIS A PAID JOB?+

No. Not Average is currently an unpaid creative community. There is no salary or guaranteed financial compensation.

DO I NEED PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE?+

No. You need a creative skill, a willingness to learn, and the ability to contribute meaningfully.

DO I HAVE TO LIVE IN LAGOS?+

Yes. The founding cohort is for people who live in Lagos, Nigeria.

HOW MANY PEOPLE ARE JOINING?+

We are starting with 10 people.

WHAT KIND OF WORK WILL WE DO?+

Creative marketing campaigns, brand concepts, content, social media projects, and other work that helps us build skills and experience.

HOW MUCH TIME DO I NEED TO COMMIT?+

You should have enough availability to participate consistently, attend team meetings, and complete the tasks you commit to. The group will agree on a practical schedule, including regular physical meetups in Lagos.

WILL I GET PAID IN THE FUTURE?+

There is no guarantee of payment. The initial focus is building experience, valuable work, and a strong creative community. Future opportunities may come from the projects and relationships we build.
